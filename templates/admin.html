1. A train running at 60 km/hr crosses a pole in 9 seconds. What is the length of the train?
   A) 120 metres
   B) 180 metres
   C) 150 metres
   D) 324 metres
Answer: C
Explanation: Speed = 60 * (5/18) m/sec = 50/3 m/sec. Length = (50/3) * 9 = 150 metres.
